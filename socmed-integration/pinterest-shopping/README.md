# pinterest shopping: the most commerce-shaped search surface

pinterest is the platform where jewelry search is already commerce
shaped: pins are catalog-backed product pins, and buyers arrive with
intent. it never had native checkout, so pins route to the pdp; for a
search-intent channel that is the natural shape.

## onboarding

- business account, claimed website (dns or html), then a catalog
  data source: platform integration or feed upload
- merchant guidelines review before product pins go live
- the verified merchant program (vmp) is **not** a tagging
  prerequisite: claimed domain + catalog + review unlocks product
  pins; vmp adds the badge and distribution. requirements include a
  returns policy, a quality site, no affiliate-only or wholesale
  operations. treat it as a post-launch goal

## tagging mechanics

- up to 24 product tags per pin; shop the look surfaces pull from the
  same catalog
- rich pins for product pages come from the same domain claim
- google merchant center is the recommended feed source, so the hub
  catalog (see [shopping-hub/](../shopping-hub/)) serves it for free

## corpus evidence (160 jewelry search pins, 2026-10-12)

- 29 percent of search results were promoted pins, 42 percent
  shoppable: paid placement is the norm, not the exception
- tall 2:3 dominated results; square appeared in only 10 of 160

## studio inputs

- photo pin: 2:3, 1000x1500 (min 600x900, max 20 mb, jpg/png/webp)
- video pin: 9:16 1080x1920 recommended (also 1:1, 2:3, 3:4, 4:5,
  1:2), 4 seconds to 15 minutes, max 2 gb
- multi-page pins (formerly idea pins): 9:16, up to 20 pages, the
  platform's reach-priority format

the missing 2:3 export preset is the studio's one pinterest-blocking
gap.
