# shopmy: the friendly door in the creator-affiliate layer

checked 2026-10-14. shopmy is the creator storefront platform that
behaviorally splits the difference between ltk's scale and amazon's
reach: creators build a curated shop (collections like "stacks i
actually wear"), drop trackable affiliate links from a network of
tens of thousands of brands, and earn brand-set commissions of
roughly 10 to 30 percent. the thing that makes it the practical
first door for a small brand is the brand side: no five-figure
subscription, brands engage creators directly through the platform
with gifting, paid partnerships, and tiered commission structures
(adjustable by sku or category).

## mechanics

- creator entry: application-based, 3 to 5 business days to review,
  or near-instant via an existing creator's referral; no hard
  follower minimum (most creators activate around 1,000 followers)
- payouts: every friday via paypal or stripe, free for creators; the
  brand side funds the platform
- snapshop browser extension: creators add products to their shop
  straight from a brand's site, which means a brand's own product
  page is the capture point - it needs to be linkable and worth
  curating
- beyond affiliate: the platform surfaces paid brand partnerships
  and gifting; creators climb a tier system that unlocks visibility
  in brand searches

## what it means for a small studio

- the affiliate motion fits made-to-order better than any checkout
  platform does: the link goes to the studio's own product page, the
  order settles on the studio's terms, and the creator's commission
  is the only price. no fixed-variant catalog, no marketplace gate
- the entry motion is gifting: send pieces to creators in the jewelry
  niche with a commission set; their storefront collections carry
  the link
- product page quality is the whole game here, which is exactly what
  the studio's deterministic renders are for: a curator screenshots
  or links what looks good on a grid
- no public api; brand and creator actions run in the shopmy app.
  nothing for the publish glue to automate; this is a relationship
  channel

## verdict

the first creator-commerce door: cheapest entry, mto-compatible
economics, and the flywheel's "product plus commission" motion in its
purest form.

evidence: ent agency creator guide (2026-07-19), shopmy.us brand and
creator pages, influencers-time platform comparison, querit searches
2026-10-14.
