# tiktok shop: the only in-app checkout in the matrix

tiktok is the most commerce-capable platform here: discovery, product
detail, payment, and fulfillment tracking all happen inside the app. it
is also the highest-friction to onboard and the one whose checkout
assumptions made-to-order goods break hardest. both things are true at
once, so this folder carries the full dossier.

## status (checked 2026-10-13)

- no follower minimum for a shop, approval in 24-48 hours
- commission by category, roughly 5-20 percent plus ~2 percent
  processing
- projected 23.41b us sales in 2026, up 48 percent year over year

## onboarding

seller center registration: us business documents, tax details, payout
bank account. brand registry or trademark matters for protecting the
listing from unauthorized resellers. listings require 5+ high
resolution images, a relevant category, clear titles and descriptions,
compliant branding, and no off-platform redirects inside shop
listings.

## tagging mechanics

- video product tags resolve to seller center listings; tags can ride
  organic posts, live shopping, and spark ads
- live shopping is the platform's strongest conversion surface
- off-platform traffic is still possible via spark ads that drive to
  the site pdp; keep the two channels deliberately separate

## the made-to-order conflict

native shop checkout cannot carry line-item options: ring size,
engraving, and plating choices have no place in a tiktok order. the
options are:

1. pre-define every option combination as a static variant sku
   (combinatorial explosion, unmanageable for custom work)
2. list simplified, no-option products natively and drive anything
   configurable to the site (loses native checkout for the custom
   work that is kirosculp's whole point)
3. skip native shop listings entirely and use tiktok as a spark-ad
   channel to the pdp

the studio's take: option 2 for fixed products if any exist, option 3
for made-to-order, re-decided when tiktok ships line-item support.

## corpus evidence (175 tagged jewelry posts, 2026-10-12)

- median 32k views, saves outrank comments ~12:1
- process and story formats win; asmr packaging and how-to dominate
- shop tags appeared in only 6 of 135 tagged posts; dm-ordering still
  outnumbers native shop tags in the niche, so the audience is trained
  on checkout-in-comments, not checkout-in-app

## studio inputs

- video 9:16, 1080x1920, up to 10 minutes; safe zone ~15 percent top
  and bottom (captions, buttons)
- photo mode: 9:16 images, up to 35 per post
- listings need 5+ images: a studiogram capture set with feed-ready
  batch export covers this in one session (see
