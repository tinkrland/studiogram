# tumblr shopping: none native, affiliate with disclosure

tumblr has no native commerce: no catalog, no tags, no checkout.
affiliate links are allowed with disclosure (the #affiliate tag),
tipping exists (5 percent platform cut), and the proven seller
categories are art prints, zines, enamel pins, jewelry.

## what tumblr is good for

- community and aesthetic depth for the craft: process posts, wip
  photos, behind-the-scenes sets in free aspect ratio (displayed
  1280 px wide, video up to 10 minutes)
- top-of-funnel discovery into the pdp via affiliate links
- repost culture that rewards long-form craft storytelling

## verdict

a low-effort, low-cost channel: repost instagram carousels at 4:5
(it accepts free aspect) and disclose affiliate links. no bespoke
studio assets, no feed work.
