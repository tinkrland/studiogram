# whatnot: live auction selling, the third in-app checkout door

checked 2026-10-14. whatnot is a live-streaming auction marketplace:
sellers go live, display items, and run real-time competitive bidding
with in-app checkout and shipping handled through the platform. it is
collectibles-led (cards, pokémon, sneakers) but runs jewelry, coin
and vintage shows as live categories. it is the third platform in the
matrix with true in-app checkout, after tiktok shop and xiaohongshu's
cross-border door.

## the money

- commission: 8 percent of the final sale price
- payment processing: about 2.9 percent plus $0.30 per order, charged
  on item price, shipping, and tax - roughly 12 percent all-in on a
  typical order
- no listing fees, no monthly subscription
- january 14, 2026: whatnot began charging 0 percent commission on
  the portion of a sale price above 1,500 usd in select categories
  (the high-value reduced fee program) - relevant if a show ever
  sells statement pieces
- payouts run on the platform's schedule after delivery
  confirmation; returns are buyer-protected through the app

## what it means for a small studio

- the operational cost is the show, not the fee: live selling needs a
  repeating schedule, in-stock inventory, and an auctioneer's pace.
  that is a real bet on a person, not a pipeline
- the mto conflict inverts: auctions need the item on the table, so
  whatnot suits in-stock pieces (samples, seconds, one-of-one
  experiments), not made-to-order. it does not break mto - it simply
  does not touch it
- the format fits the craft story the tiktok corpus validated
  (process, story, asmr) - a live bench-jewelry show is the long-form
  version of the same content
- no api for sellers beyond the platform; scheduling and listing run
  in the whatnot app. nothing for the publish glue to automate

## verdict

a someday door: in-app checkout and a real jewelry audience, but it
buys you a live schedule. revisit when there is in-stock inventory
and someone who wants to host.
