# amazon storefronts: the april 2026 update changed the shape

checked 2026-10-14. the headline: on april 14, 2026 amazon's updated
associates program operating agreement merged the influencer program
into standard associates at the entry level. every registered
associate now gets a public storefront, a custom creator link, idea
lists, shoppable photo and video uploads, and onsite earning (the
tracking ids beginning "onamz"). the application gate that used to
separate "affiliate" from "influencer" is gone at the base tier.

## the commission reality

- standard offsite rates (traffic the creator drove from outside
  amazon): fashion 4 percent, most categories 3 percent
- onsite rates (amazon served the storefront content to a shopper
  already browsing): 1 to 3 percent across categories - structurally
  lower because amazon credits its own algorithm for the traffic
- onsite product-page placements (the high-earning carousels) stay
  gated: three qualifying sales within 180 days, a content review,
  and the "original content" requirement (genuine commentary or
  transformation; slideshows of manufacturer images risk suspension)

## what it means for a small studio

- none of this applies unless the catalog is on amazon. for a
  made-to-order studio the marketplace conflict is the familiar one:
  amazon orders settle on fixed-variant listings, and mto carries no
  fixed variants. the storefront economics are a future decision
  that rides on the same catalog question as every other marketplace
- the creator side is now the low-friction door in the amazon
  ecosystem: a jewelry creator's storefront can carry a brand's
  listings with zero brand-side cost, if the brand is listed at all
- idea lists are the curation tool: themed multi-product collections
  ("everyday gold stacks") outperform single links, which is the
  same finding the pinterest and instagram corpora produced - the
  curated set is the format

## verdict

a watch item, not a door: revisit if the studio ever lists on amazon.
until then the creator-affiliate layer runs through shopmy and ltk,
where the link lands on the studio's own page.
