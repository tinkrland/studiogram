# ltk: the affiliate infrastructure, paid at the brand door

checked 2026-10-14. ltk (formerly rewardstyle and liketoknow.it,
dallas, founded 2011) is the largest independent creator-commerce
platform not owned by a social network: roughly $4 billion in
creator-driven retail sales annually, about 200,000 registered
creators, about 7,000 retail partner brands. it moves more retail
revenue through creator recommendations than any consumer platform
except tiktok shop, and it does it with less visibility than either
amazon's influencer program or shopmy's fashion-instagram coverage.

## how the money works

- affiliate commission: creators post shoppable content with
  ltk-tracked links; the retailer pays 5 to 25 percent of list price
  (beauty at the high end, mass fashion at the low end) and ltk
  takes a cut before paying the creator
- ltk sponsored: brand-paid campaigns with defined deliverables and
  fees on top of the affiliate share - the fastest-growing line, and
  the budget increasingly comes out of brand marketing rather than
  affiliate line items
- content licensing: brands buy the right to reuse creator
  photography and video in their own channels, because the creator
  post outperforms the brand asset
- ltk connect: self-serve brand access to creator discovery and
  reporting, around $12,000 per year flat (capterra listing)

## what it means for a small studio

- the brand-side door is paid and priced for established retailers:
  at roughly $12k/year for self-serve (and enterprise contracts
  above that), ltk is not a launch channel for a small jewelry
  studio. the practical route is the one the flywheel already names:
  identify creators on the platform, send product, and negotiate
  commission or a sponsored post directly
- the creator side is application-gated and skewed mid-tier and macro
  (the 50k to 2m follower band); a studio is a brand on ltk, not a
  creator
- jewelry sits inside ltk's strongest categories (women's fashion,
  beauty, home, lifestyle); creator content in the niche is abundant
- no public api for either side; everything runs through the ltk
  platform. nothing for the publish glue to automate here
- one asymmetry worth knowing: ai answer engines under-cite ltk
  relative to its transaction share (they pull from the discovery
  layer - reddit, youtube, editorial - not the affiliate layer), so
  ltk conversions do not compound into ai visibility

## verdict

sponsor-and-commission channel, not a self-serve door. park it until
creator outreach is a running habit; the same product-plus-commission
budget goes further first through shopmy, whose brand door does not
carry a five-figure entry.
