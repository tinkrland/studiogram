# youtube shopping: merchant tags on shorts, checkout on the site

youtube's shopping route for a brand is the merchant connection
(google merchant center + store), not the affiliate program: the
affiliate program is creators tagging other brands' products. both
routes matter to kirosculp, on opposite sides of the sale.

## the two routes

- **merchant route**: connect the store (shopify via google) through
  merchant center; product tags appear on shorts and long-form;
  checkout lands on the site pdp. this is the brand route
- **affiliate route**: creators in youtube's affiliate program tag
  products from participating brands and take commission. once
  kirosculp listings are in the catalog with affiliate terms set,
  third-party creators become a sales channel; youtube audiences skew
  research-driven and are skeptical of hard sells, so process content
  converts where ads do not

## tagging mechanics

- product tags need the connected catalog; tags sit on the shorts
  shelf and below long-form videos
- eligibility review happens per channel once the store is connected
- no native checkout: tags link out

## corpus evidence (45 jewelry shorts, 2026-10-13)

- median 36 seconds, median 80k views, channel median 52k subs
- asmr, tour, and process formats dominate
- commerce language appeared in only 5 of 45 descriptions; youtube
  jewelry audiences are there for the craft, which is exactly the
  studiogram story

## studio inputs

- shorts: 9:16, 1080x1920, up to 3 minutes, mp4/mov/webm, no custom
  thumbnails
- the studio's 30-second record cap covers the median exactly;
  extending past 60 seconds starts excluding instagram reels, so
  platform-targeted exports should stay under 60 for dual use
- long-form how-its-made content reuses the same records
