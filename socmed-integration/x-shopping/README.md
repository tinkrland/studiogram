# x (twitter) shopping: none, by design

x has no organic commerce surface: the shopping experiments
(twitter shopping module, x shops) were shut down in 2023, and
2026 claims of a shopping tab did not verify against first-party
sources. what remains is dynamic product ads (from a feed, via the
ad platform) and link-out.

## what x is good for

- broadcast and cultural-relevance posts with a link out to the pdp
- real-time craft commentary, process clips, replies to the niche
- x video: 16:9 (1280x720 min) or 1:1, max 2:20 standard, 512 mb

## corpus evidence (800 jewelry-tag posts, 2026-10-13)

bot-heavy sample, median 3 likes: x is a weak organic channel for
this niche. allocate effort accordingly - a share of the 16:9 export
and nothing bespoke.
