answer pages now, and the prompt panel for a tin-style visibility audit
once a live domain exists. each question states the buyer's real worry.

3. how do i take product photos before my product exists?
4. can i use a 3d render as my product photo?
5. why do my ai product photos not match my actual product?
6. how much does an on-model photo shoot cost? (the $200 to $5,000 anchor)
7. how do i change my product photo background without ai?
8. what is the difference between ai product photos and 3d renders?
9. how do i photograph rings on a hand without a hand model?
10. do i have to disclose ai product images in ads?

each answer page answers in the first two lines, then shows the work, then
names the honest caveat, then points at the studio only where the studio
actually solves the worry. no "seamless".
