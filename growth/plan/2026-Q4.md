# growth plan, 2026-Q4

sized to a pre-launch studio with no live domain. compose now, publish at
launch, measure after. nothing here executes anything.

## before the domain is live (now)

1. owner rulings, in order: the explicit-fee decision on the sculptura
   side does not block this folder; the hand-model license ruling does
   block any public imagery. cc by-sa 3.0 permits commercial use with
   attribution; the ruling to make is whether porcelain-finish
   presentation satisfies attribution fidelity in practice. the model
   credit is already rendered in the ui.
2. enable github pages (settings, deploy from main, root). that is the
   whole deploy. then point sculptura.ltd at it if the studio stands
   alone.
3. upgrade the four platform-primary sources in the deep dive from
   snippet to full read, then the two answer pages are publishable.
4. compose the comparison page skeleton: studiogram vs diffusion on-model
   tools, one row per structural difference (input, exactness, tweaks,
   flags, cost per retake). no competitor claims without a receipt.

## at launch

5. publish the two answer pages and the positioning page first. they
   answer questions buyers already ask in search and in ai assistants.
6. wire search console. then the weekly loop can start: what moved
   becomes next week's plan.
7. the visibility audit asks the ten buyer questions in
   buyer-questions.md, with and without search.

## after there is traffic

8. keyword plan from search console reality; content plan sized to
   review time the owner actually has (start: one review hour a week).
9. the burned-seller segment becomes reachable only with a mesh onramp;
   keep the answer pages working for them in the meantime.

## never

- no publishing to any platform without owner approval. this folder
  composes; it does not ship.
- no "no ai label needed" marketing. the position is honest disclosure
  with exact rendering, never label evasion.
